## What changed on the site

1. Every page on one layout: the disclaimer at the top and bottom, navigation, link preview tags and a Markdown twin.
2. **Portugal now** and **Warnings**: a map of the 25 warning areas, built in the browser from IPMA's 278 municipality points (so no map geometry is in the repository), with "In force now", Today, Tomorrow and Day after; the warnings' timeline and text; MeteoAlarm's CAP feed as context with its own terms.
3. **Forecasts** (35 places, one scale), **Stations** (on the map, coloured by temperature, with wind arrows and a sortable table), **Fire risk** (by municipality, three days), **Sea** (swell and total sea, labelled as in IPMA's file), **UV** (WHO bands, every value written).
4. Beside every chart: IPMA as the source, the read time, how stale, the card's terms, and the collector's recent runs. When a file is missing, slow, stale or old, the page says so above the chart and shows no value in its place.
5. **Storm Charles** as an archived event, summarising PrompTugal.news's page with the Lisbon alert levels drawn only from the times it states and the timeline with sources. **How it works** as a diagram. **Use with AI** and `llms.txt`.
6. **Behind the scenes**, generated at build time from `team/` and git log (6593d19, built ahead for round 2).
