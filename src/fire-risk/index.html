Fire risk

IPMA gives each mainland municipality a fire risk class (RCM, risco de incêndio rural) from 1, reduzido, to 5, máximo, for today and the next two days. For rules on burning and the use of fire, follow ANEPC and your municipality, not this page.

This page needs JavaScript: it reads the fire risk from data.promptugal.news.
