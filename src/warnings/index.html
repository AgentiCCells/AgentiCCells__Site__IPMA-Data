Warnings

IPMA issues weather warnings for the 18 mainland districts, four areas of Madeira and the three island groups of the Azores, at four levels: green, yellow, orange and red. This page draws the warnings in PrompTugal.news's latest copy of IPMA's file. It can be behind IPMA: check ipma.pt before acting on anything.

Context: MeteoAlarm's feed for Portugal

MeteoAlarm (EUMETNET) republishes Portugal's warnings as CAP messages. They come from IPMA, the national weather service, and are shown here as context beside IPMA's own file, not as a second source of truth. MeteoAlarm's times are UTC.
