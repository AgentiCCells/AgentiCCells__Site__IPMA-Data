Weather warnings

IPMA issues weather warnings for 18 mainland districts, for areas of Madeira and for the groups of islands of the Azores, at four levels: green, yellow, orange and red. This page draws the warnings in PrompTugal.news's latest copy of IPMA's file. It can be behind IPMA; check ipma.pt before acting on anything.
