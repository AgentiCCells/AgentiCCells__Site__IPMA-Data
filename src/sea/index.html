Sea: waves and sea temperature

IPMA's forecast for the coast, today and the next two days: how high the sea and the swell will be, the swell's period, the direction of the waves and the temperature of the sea surface. All places share one height scale.

The total sea is IPMA's significant wave height: wind waves and swell together. The swell is the part that arrives from distant weather. For conditions at sea, see IPMA's coastal forecast and warnings.

When the collector read IPMA

The sea forecast is read from IPMA by PrompTugal.news's collector. Each mark is one of its runs; a run that found new bytes from IPMA is marked as new data.
